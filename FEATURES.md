Public Speaking Preparation

Normal Mode
- Computer Vision (OpenCV) - to judge body language, hand gestures, etc.
- (Additional, not necessary) - people looking at you
- Be able to give context to your speech beforehand
- Listen to what you're saying
- Gemini creates responses, ElevenLabs voices them
    - Impromptu questions about the speech based on whats been said and the context beforehand
    - Formalities
- At the end, should create a reflection highlighting what can be improved, use ElevenLabs to say it

Improv Mode
- Picks a random topic, webscrapes for information on that topic
- Gives you some information / speaking points on that topic
- Speak for a certain amount of time
- It gives similar feedback to the normal mode
